Our Heritage

A Tradition of Exquisite Taste & Warmth

From our humble beginnings in 2002 as an ambitious kitchen in Nairobi, The Amazon Restaurant has blossomed into one of Kenya's most revered culinary destinations. We treat every guest who walks through our doors as beloved family.

Beyond our exquisite dining suites, we offer serene spa and rejuvenation treatments, expansive executive conference spaces, and personalized catering tailored to every celebration.
