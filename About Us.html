The Beginning

Our Story

They say every great achievement begins with a dream; for us, it began as an inspiring story. Founded in the year 2002, The Amazon Restaurant was born out of a genuine passion for bringing people together over fresh, flavorful dishes inspired by timeless culinary traditions and vibrant local culture.

What started as an intimate kitchen with boundless ambitions has grown into a prestigious, welcoming sanctuary where every guest is honored as family. Proudly rooted in the heart of Westlands, Nairobi, we have expanded to multiple premier branches nationwide, led by visionary chefs and hospitality professionals dedicated to perfection in every encounter.

Every meal created in our kitchen is a symphony of flavor, celebration of community, and an enduring tribute to the fine art of dining.

Wellness & Hospitality

Our Curated Services

Beyond our world-class dining, The Amazon Resort offers holistic wellness and event services crafted to pamper your senses and soothe the spirit. Our dedicated Spa & Rejuvenation Center provides treatments specifically engineered to relieve stress, improve circulation, and enhance your overall well-being.

Our North Star

Our Mission & Culinary Ethos

Our mission is to consistently deliver an unparalleled dining experience that blends extraordinary culinary creativity, attentive five-star service, and a warm, sophisticated ambiance.

We are deeply committed to sustainable and responsible gastronomy: sourcing 100% of our vegetables, herbs, dairy, and poultry from local Kenyan organic farms, and our seafood straight from fresh sustainable waters.

Furthermore, we actively champion our local Nairobi community through culinary apprentice programs, food sustainability partnerships, and charitable community outreach.

The Amazon Distinction

Why Choose Amazon Restaurant

At Amazon Restaurant, we focus on what truly counts: wholesome and uncompromised ingredients, authentic rich flavors, and unforgettable hospitality. Every plate that leaves our kitchen is a testament to the mastery of our culinary artisans.

Whether you are stopping by for a brisk business lunch, celebrating a milestone anniversary with loved ones, or unwinding with artisanal cocktails, we guarantee consistent quality, prompt courteous service, and an atmosphere you will long to return to.
